Pass 2 — Separations

Every color is its own screen.

Your art gets split into one plate per ink. We burn each plate onto a mesh screen, 110 to 230 threads per inch depending on the detail, line the screens up on four registration points, and pull them one after another. If the points are off by a hair, you see it. Ours are not.

PLATES: C M Y K · REGISTER: 0 PX · 156 MESH

CYAN PLATE156 MESH15°
MAGENTA PLATE156 MESH75°
YELLOW PLATE156 MESH0°
BLACK PLATE230 MESH45°

Coarser mesh, bigger dots. Finer mesh holds finer dots. Four angles keep four plates from forming moiré.

Art we love

Vector (AI, PDF, SVG) or 300 dpi at print size. Tell us a PMS number and we mix to it, a Pantone Solid Coated swatch, not a screen. A phone photo of a sketch works too; we redraw it and send a proof before anything is burned.

Questions

Things people ask before they order.

How few shirts can I order?1 piece · DTF

One. Under 12 pieces we usually go DTF so you are not paying to burn screens for a handful.

Forty shirts for the church picnic, three kids' sizes, by Friday?Screen · 2 color · 156 mesh · mixed sizes OK

Sizes can mix freely inside one run. Give us the date and the art and we tell you the same day whether Friday is real.

Screen print or DTF, which is better?Screen for runs · DTF for photos

Screen for big runs, spot colors and the softest hand on water-based ink. DTF for photos, gradients and tiny runs.

Will you match my school colors?PMS match · Solid Coated

Give us the PMS numbers, or a sample, and we mix to it.

Are the stickers waterproof?651 · laminated · 6 yr outdoor

Printed on vinyl, laminated, kiss-cut. Dishwashers and Houston summers, yes.

How big can a sign be?Banner 10 ft · coroplast 4×8 · ACM 4×8

13oz banners to 10 feet on one piece, coroplast to 4×8 feet, aluminum composite the same.

What file should I send?Vector or 300 dpi at size

Vector if you have it. If not, the biggest PNG or PDF you have, and we will tell you if it will hold up.
