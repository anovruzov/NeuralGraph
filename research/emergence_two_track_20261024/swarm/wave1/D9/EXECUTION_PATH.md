## Verification of the two prior-audit hypotheses

### H1 "inert coordinator"

Object: `NeuralGraph.research.coordination.core.TesseractCoordinator` (`core.py:380-471`), constructed in `Hq.__init__` (`hq.py:379-380`) with `Router(self.registry, failure)`, `ClaimNormalizer()`, `RuleBasedSynthesizer(("verdict",))`, `LineageAnalyzer()`, `TraceLogger`; replaced in `E2EHq.__init__` (`hq_tesseract.py:69-71`) by an identical one whose `Router` holds the kept `FailureInjector`. Invoked once per (question, arm) at `hq.py:423-424` with `preferred_node_ids = asked.orders["R"]` and `QueryBudget(max_nodes=3, max_claims=3, max_verifications=0)` (`hq.py:418-421`).

Decisions it makes on the path:
1. **Route** — `Router.select` (`core.py:230-241`): `eligible = registry.eligible_nodes(request, failure)` (206-222: descriptor offers the capability, availability not `unavailable`, `failure.route_available`), then `preferred ∩ eligible` in preferred order + the rest, cut to `max_nodes`. The *order* is HQ's (`hq.plan` → `order_for("R")`, `hq.py:141-167`, RRF over four cell-derived signals, `93-115`); the coordinator only filters and cuts. The harness pre-computes the same cut (`route_of`, `hq_tesseract.py:206-212`) and the report asserts `routes_equal_arm_order` (`harness.py:609,656`; `test_harness.py:689`). The filter changes a route only under the drill-2 mask (`probe_masked`, `hq_tesseract.py:86-103`; `seed-0.log:6`).
2. **Synthesis/fragility** — `normalize` (`core.py:244-257`), `synthesize` (260-293), `score` (303-365) run and emit trace events (441-456) but **no code in `research/` reads `execution.synthesis`, `.claims` or `.metrics`** (grep); `hq.py:436-438` reads only `execution.route` and `execution.exported_bytes`. The status is `gate.evaluate(...)` (`hq.py:435`) over `self.proxies[s].last[qid]` (432), i.e. the raw intake bodies, not the coordinator's claims.
3. **Verification/repair** — never: `max_verifications=0`; `SiteProxyAdapter.verify` is a stub returning `valid=False, reason="not used by the spike"` (`hq.py:316-319`); `RepairPlanner` is instantiated only in `NeuralGraph/research/coordination/experiment.py` and `NeuralGraph/tests/test_coordination.py`.

Verdict: **partly confirmed, precisely**: the coordinator is a route-cutter and trace-emitter. It makes exactly one decision (the eligibility-filtered cut of an order HQ already fixed), and its two analytic outputs are dead on this path. It is not "inert" in the sense of unexecuted — `coordinator_class`, `router_class`, `route_selected_first_all` and the per-question trace are report-checked (`harness.py:654-657,623-625`; `test_harness.py:683-699`).

### H2 "evidence truncation" (site → HQ)

Points inspected for slicing, top-k, caps and `MAX_*`:
* **Site read cap L=50** (the one real truncation): `MAX_RECORDS = 50` (`emergence_world.py:90`) → `SiteConfig.max_records` (438-443) → `TesseractRetrieval._max` (`site_process.py:206`). `entity_matched` is uncapped (`cap=max(1, own)`, 303-313), then `shipped = matched[:self._max]` (323); `union` caps the total at L (`292-301`: `room = L - len(shipped)`, `others[:room]`); `QueryBudget(max_claims=L)` (328) and the adapter stops at `len(exports) >= max_claims` (`adapters.py:329-330`). `truncated = unread > 0` where `unread` = entity-matched in-window records not read (334, 341) — i.e. true exactly when more than 50 records match.
